Mixed reality for Meta Quest 3 and 3S

Squash against your own wall.

Your headset finds the real walls in your room. Pick one, and it becomes the front wall of a squash court. The ball bounces off your actual walls, floor and furniture.

Get playing

  1. Open this page on your Quest

    Use the Meta Quest Browser. Pick a mode and level, press "Play in your room", and allow access to your room when the headset asks.

  2. Choose your wall

    Point a controller at a clear wall. The court lines appear on it. Pull the trigger to play there.

  3. Hit the ball at the wall

    A ball floats in front of you. Swing through it to serve, then keep the rally going.

Controls

Swing
Each controller holds a bat. Swing harder to hit harder.
Trigger
Pick the wall, choose in the menu, or start a new game.
A or X
New ball in front of you, any time.
B or Y
Open the menu: new ball, court size, colour, mode, level, another wall.
Thumbstick
Left and right: court width. Up and down: court height.
Draw the court
In the menu, choose "Draw the court". Point at one corner, hold the trigger, drag to the opposite corner and let go.
Whole wall
Pick "Whole wall" as the court size, or turn on "Fill the whole wall" in the menu.
Bare hands
Put the controllers down. Hit with your open palm. Quick pinch: new ball. Pinch and hold: menu.

The Quest's own ≡ button belongs to the headset, so web games cannot use it. The game pauses while the Quest menu is open and shows its own menu when you come back.

The rules

Score
Your shot reaches the front wall: 1 point, times your multiplier.
Multiplier
Every 4 returns in a row adds ×1, up to ×4. A lost ball resets it.
The tin
The low line on the wall. Hit below it and you lose the ball.
Bounces
Hit the ball before it bounces twice. Casual lets it bounce twice before you must hit it.
Out lines
Keep your shot inside the side lines and under the top line.
Target
Hit the ring for a bonus: 3 points in Rally, 5 in Target attack, times your multiplier.

The game uses your Quest's Space Setup to find walls, the floor and tables. If your room has not been set up, you play against a virtual wall placed where you point. Clear the space around you before you swing.
